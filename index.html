Steger Family Daily Text

Steger Family Daily Text is a daily messaging service that sends one short message each morning drawn from the writing of a late loved one: a quote from his stories and letters, a song he shared, or a book he recommended. It is currently in a pilot with members of the Steger family, sharing the words of the late Arthur “Buzz” Steger, and is being developed so other families can create the same for someone they have lost.
