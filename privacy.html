Privacy Policy

Steger Family Daily Text · Effective November 8, 2026

This policy explains how Steger Family Daily Text, operated by Brooke Steger, sole proprietor, handles information for its text message service.

Information we collect

Your mobile phone number, the date you opted in, and a record of messages sent and of your STOP or HELP replies. If you give it, your first name.

How we use it

Only to send you the daily Steger Family Daily Text message, to confirm your subscription, and to respond to STOP and HELP requests.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your phone number is shared only with our messaging provider, Twilio, for the sole purpose of delivering messages.

Retention and deletion

We keep your number while you are subscribed. If you reply STOP or ask us to delete your information, we remove it from our list.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: brookesteger@gmail.com
